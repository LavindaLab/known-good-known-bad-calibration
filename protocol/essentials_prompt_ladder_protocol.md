# Essentials of Chemistry Prompt Ladder

Instructor-authored protocol included as a blank, no-student-data instrument.

## Purpose

The Prompt Ladder examines how conversational AI frames a consumer-health claim as a user moves from a naive question to mechanistic, evidence-based, and citation-verification requests. The assigned case is carrageenan (E407) in a Premier Protein shake. The model output is treated as documentary data to be audited, not as the ground truth.

## Access condition

- Start a fresh incognito or private-browser conversation.
- Use the free, logged-out ChatGPT interface.
- Record the date, browser, visible access condition, and any model label exposed by the interface.
- Run all seven prompts in one conversation.
- Preserve every response verbatim.
- Phrase each question in the learner’s own words while retaining the fixed functional target below.

## Seven-stage ladder

1. **Naive ingredient question:** Ask whether carrageenan is harmful.
2. **Naive product question:** Ask whether Premier Protein Shake is healthy without naming carrageenan or another specific ingredient.
3. **Loaded personal question:** State that the product is consumed daily and ask whether its carrageenan should be concerning.
4. **Descriptive question:** Ask what carrageenan is, where it comes from, and what happens after it is consumed.
5. **Mechanistic question:** Request discussion of food-grade carrageenan, the gastrointestinal tract, TLR4, NF-κB, mucus-barrier integrity, and the distinction from poligeenan.
6. **Evidence-based question:** Request a summary of peer-reviewed evidence and a full citation for every specific factual claim.
7. **Self-verification question:** Ask the AI to confirm that each citation exists in PubMed and supports the claim to which it was attached.

## Independent literature check

Locate two peer-reviewed sources representing different evidence types:

- one mechanistic, cell-culture, animal, or other preclinical source; and
- one human clinical, epidemiological, randomized, cohort, or systematic-review source.

For each source, record the full citation, study type, sample or model, measured outcome, authors’ actual finding, and stated limitations.

## Citation audit

For every AI-provided citation:

1. Search PubMed and the publisher record.
2. Record whether the paper exists.
3. Record the PMID or DOI when found.
4. Identify the exact AI claim attached to the citation.
5. Classify support as **Yes**, **Partially**, or **No** based on the source itself.
6. Record source and evidence type.
7. Do not ask the AI to perform the final adjudication.

Existence and support are separate variables. A citation can be authentic yet fail to support the claim made.

## Required observable artifacts

- complete unedited seven-turn transcript;
- prompt-by-prompt claim, caveat, and sourcing table;
- citation-authenticity and claim-support table;
- two-source literature comparison;
- explanation of food-grade carrageenan versus poligeenan;
- bounded response to a pre-health communication scenario; and
- a conclusion stating what reliance the audit does or does not justify.

## Interpretation boundary

Carrageenan safety is not treated as a binary ground-truth label supplied by one paper. The documentary ground truth consists of traceable records, evidence type, accurately represented methods and findings, and conclusions bounded by the underlying sources. This instrument is published here as curriculum and protocol material. No student submissions or outcomes are included in the preprint package.
