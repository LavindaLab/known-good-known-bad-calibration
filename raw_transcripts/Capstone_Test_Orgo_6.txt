You must see:
	•	Strong C=O stretch ~1700–1715 cm⁻¹
	•	Very broad O–H from ~2500–3300 cm⁻¹
👉 So if your report truly lacks a strong ~1700 cm⁻¹ peak, then:
	•	either the IR was misread,
	•	or the spectrum is not adipic acid (or is contaminated / incorrectly assigned).
